See completed example of [Content and Art Direction](https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle--example-?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=wGYWquiNFC8Ob4Cw-0)

## Setup <a href="#pick-a-topic" id="pick-a-topic"></a>

* Pick any topic you would like to animate
* Create a new Figma file with **2-pages** titled
  * _**Art Direction**_
  * _**SVG Illustration**_

## Define Art Direction <a href="#find-content-and-imagery" id="find-content-and-imagery"></a>

* Find content for this topic. Words, images, textures, patterns, etc.
* Put this content to the page _**Art Direction**_
  * &#x20;[See example Art Direction.](https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle-\(example\)?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=mAFZj3rPwvaXPxXd-0)​

### Create SVG Illustration

* Create and/or find SVG illustrations for your topic
* Put this content to the page _**SVG Illustration**_
* Place SVG illustrations onto a **single-frame** aka the `svg` parent with many children
* Ensure your designs support your theme and Art Direction
  * [See example Goodle Doodle SVG](https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle-\(example\)?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=mAFZj3rPwvaXPxXd-0) Illustratoin
