See completed example from Lectures : [https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle-(example)?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=mAFZj3rPwvaXPxXd-0](https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle-\(example\)?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=mAFZj3rPwvaXPxXd-0)​

#### Pick a Topic <a href="#pick-a-topic" id="pick-a-topic"></a>

* Pick any topic you like.
* Are you familiar with the topic?
* Can you work with it for many weeks?

#### Find Content and Imagery <a href="#find-content-and-imagery" id="find-content-and-imagery"></a>

* Find text content for this topic
  * Write 50+ words about it. [See example.](https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle-\(example\)?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=mAFZj3rPwvaXPxXd-0)​
* Find imagery i.e. images, textures, patterns, etc.
  * Create a moodboard. [See example.](https://www.figma.com/design/KIEiC0Tgx5IF1BhpTLZhn0/Google-Doodle-\(example\)?node-id=0-1\&p=f\&t=mAFZj3rPwvaXPxXd-0)​

#### Determine an Art Direction <a href="#determine-an-art-direction" id="determine-an-art-direction"></a>

> Design with Direction is _decoration_. Design ≠ _decoration_.

* Read [Art Direction and Design by Dan Mall](https://alistapart.com/article/art-direction-and-design/)​
* Visually, how are the following achieved?
  * What is mood/tone?
  * What is the composition/layout?
  * What is the concept?
